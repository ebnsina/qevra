# Changelog

## Unreleased

First version.

- Public website for the academy: home, courses, course pages with a free preview, reviews, resources, blog and text pages
- Student accounts with email and password, and password reset by email
- Enrollment by bKash or Nagad with staff confirmation; free courses start at once
- Lessons with video, notes, quizzes and assignments; live classes; progress and certificates
- Admin: payments, students, assignment review, courses and lessons, and the website's content
- Courses can be listed as "Coming soon" before they open for enrollment
- On phones, the portal and admin have a bottom tab bar like a mobile app
- Fixed: a student enrolled in a course with no lessons yet saw "page not found"; they now see that lessons are being added, with any live classes
- Lesson notes can contain tables
- Fixed: the logo mark was cut off when the sidebar was collapsed
- Students: questions and answers under each lesson, private notes, lesson downloads, and course ratings and reviews
- Teacher: Questions, Course reviews, Analytics and Coupons screens; announcements to a course (in the portal and by email); drag sections and lessons into order; preview a course as a student
- Courses can show a regular fee struck through, and students can enter a coupon code when enrolling
- Posts, pages and course details open in a full editor page with a rich text editor, instead of a side sheet
- Overview pages for staff and students: numbers, a 30-day chart, what is waiting, latest activity
- Analytics: choose 30 days, 90 days or 12 months; trends for enrollments, income, new students, lessons and quiz tries; income by course, how students pay, ratings, hardest quizzes and most active students
- Colored icons in the dashboard; forest green is the default brand color
- Website: courses can be filtered (open now, free, coming soon); reviews, resources, blog, posts and the contact page are redesigned, and every page ends with one next step
- The seed now loads a generic sample academy instead of one academy's own content, and can add demo activity
- Renamed to Tadris
- Website headings are set in Fraunces, a serif
- Sora headings and Cabin text across the website and the dashboard, with bold website headings; blue is the default brand color, the same on light and dark
- Corners now scale with the size of what they round; cards and the header lift with shadows instead of outlines; the banner's grid pattern is gone
- Course and post covers are photographs; the closing band has an aurora gradient
- The website header blends into each page's opening section and gains its own surface on scroll
- The sample academy is named Lisan Academy
- Sign-in pages show a photo beside the form on wide screens
- Strength, step and resource cards show their icon as a large watermark
- Contact page: WhatsApp and email buttons and common questions
- Every input shows an example of what to type
- Phone number is required when creating an account
- "Forgot password?" is gone from the sign-in page; staff set a new password for a student from the Students screen
- Overview and Analytics are split into tabs instead of one long page; number tiles are tinted in their color
- Dates, times, colors and dropdowns in forms use the app's own pickers instead of the browser's
