- On phones, the portal and admin have a bottom tab bar like a mobile app
- Fixed: a student enrolled in a course with no lessons yet saw "page not found"; they now see that lessons are being added, with any live classes
- Lesson notes can contain tables
- Fixed: the logo mark was cut off when the sidebar was collapsed
- Students: questions and answers under each lesson, private notes, lesson downloads, and course ratings and reviews
- Teacher: Questions, Course reviews, Analytics and Coupons screens; announcements to a course (in the portal and by email); drag sections and lessons into order; preview a course as a student
- Courses can show a regular fee struck through, and students can enter a coupon code when enrolling
